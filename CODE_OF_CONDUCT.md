# Code of Conduct

## Welcome

I want this to be a friendly and respectful space where everyone feels comfortable learning, asking questions, and sharing ideas.

## A Few Things to Keep in Mind

- **Be kind and respectful.** Everyone is at a different stage of their learning journey. Be patient and treat others the way you'd like to be treated.
- **Keep feedback constructive.** If you spot an error, broken walkthrough, or upstream sync mismatch, explain why. Healthy discussion is always welcome.
- **Help keep things useful.** Stay on topic and avoid spam or unnecessary promotion.

## What Doesn't Belong Here

A few things have no place in this community:

- Harassment, bullying, insults, or personal attacks.
- Sharing private information, personal Anki collection data, or credentials.
- Spam, inappropriate content, or repeated off-topic promotion.

## Reporting an Issue

If something doesn't feel right or you come across behavior that goes against these guidelines, please let me know.

You can reach me at [saadrehman.med@gmail.com](mailto:saadrehman.med@gmail.com).

I'll do my best to look into it and handle the situation fairly and respectfully.

## One Last Thing

This project is built for learning and helping Anki users navigate the manual easily. The goal is simple: keep official advice faithful and help each other.

Thanks for being here!
